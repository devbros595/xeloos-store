eSIM INSTALLATION GUIDE

How to Install & Activate Your eSIM

Get connected in minutes. Follow the steps below to install your Xeloos eSIM and activate your mobile data.

What is an eSIM

Know what an eSIM is before installation.

An eSIM, short for embedded SIM, is a revolutionary technology that aims to simplify the way we use mobile devices. Unlike traditional physical SIM cards, which require a physical swap when changing carriers or devices, eSIMs are built directly into the device. This means there's no need to struggle anymore with tiny cards or worry about losing them. An eSIM is a simple QR code which just need to be scanned to be install.

Additionally, eSIM technology offers robust security measures that are on par with or even superior to traditional physical SIM cards. eSIMs support remote provisioning and management, which means that the activation and deactivation processes can be securely controlled by the network operator. This reduces the risk of SIM swapping attacks and unauthorized SIM transfers.

Before you start

Make sure your device is ready for eSIM installation.

eSIM-compatible device

Confirm that your phone supports eSIM functionality.

Internet connection

Connect to Wi-Fi or another working internet connection before installing your eSIM.

Your eSIM details

Keep your Xeloos eSIM QR code or activation details available.

Enough battery

Make sure your device has enough battery to complete the installation.

Install on iPhone

Follow these steps on your iPhone.

STEP 1
Open Settings

Open Settings on your iPhone and select Mobile Service or Cellular.

STEP 2
Add an eSIM

Tap Add eSIM or Add Cellular Plan.

STEP 3
Scan your QR code

Select Use QR Code and scan the QR code supplied with your Xeloos eSIM.

STEP 4
Add the mobile plan

Follow the instructions on screen and confirm that you want to add the mobile plan.

STEP 5
Choose your eSIM

When prompted, choose your Xeloos eSIM as the line you want to use for mobile data.

STEP 6
Enable Data Roaming

If required by your eSIM plan, open the eSIM's mobile settings and enable Data Roaming.

Install on Android

Menu names may vary depending on your device manufacturer.

STEP 1
Open Settings

Open Settings and go to Connections, Network & Internet, or Mobile Network.

STEP 2
Add an eSIM

Look for an option such as Add eSIM, Add Mobile Plan, or SIM Manager.

STEP 3
Scan your QR code

Choose the QR-code option and scan the QR code provided with your Xeloos eSIM.

STEP 4
Download the eSIM

Follow the prompts to download and install the mobile plan on your device.

STEP 5
Select the eSIM for data

Set your Xeloos eSIM as the preferred SIM for mobile data.

STEP 6
Enable Data Roaming

If your plan requires it, enable Data Roaming for the Xeloos eSIM.

ACTIVATION

How to activate your eSIM

Installation and activation can depend on the eSIM provider and destination. In most cases, your eSIM will connect once you arrive at the supported destination and enable the line.

1. Turn on the eSIM

Make sure your Xeloos eSIM line is switched on in your device's mobile network settings.

2. Connect to the network

Once you're within the supported coverage area, your eSIM should connect to an available network.

3. Start using your data

Select the Xeloos eSIM for mobile data and start browsing, messaging, and using your apps.

Important

Do not delete your eSIM after installation unless you are certain that you no longer need it. Some eSIMs cannot be reinstalled after being removed from a device.

Having trouble connecting?

Confirm that the Xeloos eSIM is selected for mobile data, mobile data is enabled, and data roaming is enabled if required by your plan.

Make sure your device supports eSIM and that you have a stable internet connection. Check that the QR code or activation details are being entered correctly.

Contact the Xeloos support team with your order details and the device you are using so we can assist you.
